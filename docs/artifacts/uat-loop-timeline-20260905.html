The run2026-07-04 to 2026-07-05

Twenty-four findings in about thirty hours

The first walkthrough of the Music app shared with other people. The operator tested on his phone and sent what he saw - short bursts, screenshots, one of them with a typo in it. Twenty-four findings landed, numbered U1 to U24.
What makes this worth telling is not that they were fixed. It is what happened to them afterward.
Nine of the twenty-four, in order. Every quote is the operator, verbatim.
U1operator2026-07-04

The one they did not fix

"the built-in songbook doesn't deliver for true play/perform"typed "play/pedom" on a phone, read as play/perform
Nothing shipped. The finding was routed to a vision interview as a product-direction item, with four probe questions written before anyone touched code: what happened in the moment, which app or paper workflow the user reaches for instead, whether the unit is a song sheet or a progression, and how hands-free it has to be.
A loop that patches everything cannot tell a bug from a wrong product. This one stopped.

U8shipped2026-07-04

Shipped, gated, measured

"when count chords over width, convert to existing smaller chord icon without chart. wrap chords to keep in view without scroll horiz."
Width-measured threshold, flex-wrap, no horizontal scroll. Shipped the same day as S-PROG-WRAP, and the gate was a number rather than a look: composeTop measured 379px of the 915px budget with the twelve-bar loaded.
Nine words of phone-speak became a measured acceptance condition.

U8boperator2026-07-04

The screenshot that reopened it

"make 6 per row so no 3rd row wrap. degrade at 8, 10...? keep 3 and 4 chord songs to fill box... keep logical count per row"a follow-up screenshot at 11 chords, hours after U8 shipped
The root cause was named rather than worked around: the shipped threshold was width-only, and flex-wrap has no concept of a logical count per row, so eleven chords broke as an uneven 5 + 5 + 1 with a mostly-empty third row. The replacement is count-driven - full diagram cards at 1-4, one compact row at 5-6, and a fixed six-column grid at 7-12, so twelve is exactly two rows of six and a third orphan row cannot happen.
A second finding on the same feature bought a better model, not a bigger patch.

U9diagnosis2026-07-04

The bug nobody was hunting

"The added to setlist toast confirmation never disappears - navigated to my setlist and it's still showing."
The cause was two lines apart in the same closure: var toastTimer declared twice, once around line 1454 and again around 2441. Hoisting makes them one variable, so the two toast systems were clobbering each other's hide timer. It had been there all along - new traffic from a truthful-toast change is what finally made it visible.
The fix was extraction into a shared primitive, not a patch on the symptom. UAT found a latent defect no test was looking for.

U10became a rule2026-07-04

The finding that became a law

"I like the guidance... but it camouflaged with the highlight rows. Make a prim and enforce consistency - for each element in each view in the whole app so I don't have to type that again."
Guidance was wearing selection's clothes: the educational banner and the in-setlist row highlight shared one pale accent surface. The fix went to the primitive - notables get their own surface token, app-wide. But the last clause is the part that outlived the bug. It was encoded as THE ELEMENT CONSISTENCY LAW in the repo's working notes and the SSOT registry, and consistency findings now route to primitives by law.
He said it once. The system remembers it instead of him. This is the whole point of the loop, in one finding.

U18direction2026-07-04

A north star fell out of a test session

"I put that bonus on all our users so they can compound the time it took us... skip the empty-library phase and move immediately on their journey."
Not a defect. Mid-walkthrough, the operator specified a product strategy: curate a playlist across the mode and scale matrix, bake it into the shipped catalog, and let every new user start with a ready jam library instead of an empty one.
A UAT session is supposed to find bugs. This one produced the roadmap.

U22diagnosis2026-07-05

He offered an easy out. The loop declined it.

"using next/previous through the songs of the set, we're losing something for the current song and getting an unexpected message. Could be data specific to me but..."
It was not his data. Deleting a library item leaves its id behind in any setlist that held it, queue navigation resolves the ghost to nothing, and the perform pane falls through to the library empty state while the counter still counts the missing song. A dangling reference is a class, so the fix went in at three levels: the delete flow heals the references, navigation skips unresolvable entries with a calm notice, and load-time pruning catches whatever already rotted.
The operator hedged. The diagnosis did not.

U23became a rule2026-07-05

A teaching moment became a principle

"The guidance says this scale works because F major and its relative minor are the same notes - but doesn't say WHAT the minor key is. Valuable insight... a message on the continuum of your journey, available at point of need."
The copy named a relationship and left the reader to compute the instance. The fix interpolates the concrete name from the key already in hand. The principle went into the design conventions: name the instance, not just the relationship - the concrete name is the point-of-need teaching.
One sentence of guidance copy, and the rule behind it now applies to every sentence of guidance copy.

U24shipped2026-07-05

It ends with a test

"delete song from setlist leaves confirmation container empty. was saved quickly."
A lifecycle seam between two pull requests that merged hours apart - one owning toast host semantics, the other a setlist undo banner - left the host rendered with nothing in it. The repair unifies the path onto the toast-plus-action primitive. What matters more is what ships beside it: a regression test asserting that no visible-empty-host state exists after any toast lifecycle completes, on every host and every path.
The last finding of the run closes its own class behind it.
