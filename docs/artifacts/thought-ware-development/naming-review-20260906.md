# Methodology Naming Review

Date: 2026-09-06  
Status: preliminary collision screen; not a trademark or legal clearance

## Decision

Do not lock a public methodology name in v0.1. Keep **Thought-Ware Development** as a
provisional project umbrella and use **friction-to-goalpost loop** as the descriptive
name of the method's operational core.

The earlier candidate **Evidence-Gated Agentic Engineering** is withdrawn because
“Evidence-Gated Agentic Development” is already in public use for a highly similar AI
engineering pattern.

## Collision findings

| Term | Finding | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| Thoughtware / Thought-Ware | Used publicly for intent-centric AI software and other established meanings | Keep only as provisional umbrella pending clearance |
| Evidence-Gated Agentic Engineering | Near-identical Evidence-Gated Agentic Development already published | Withdraw |
| User-Centric Development | Generic and close to established human-centred design terminology | Use descriptively, not as a distinctive research claim |
| SDD | Overloaded by Spec-Driven, Simulation-Driven, Song-Driven, and other methods | Do not use as the standalone public name |
| User-Simulation-Driven Development | Descriptive but user simulation is an established research field; “SDD” remains ambiguous | Retain only as a component label with definition |
| Goal-Driven Development | Existing generic software-development usage | Avoid as primary name |
| Evidence-Bound Development | “EvidenceBound” is already used in runtime-assurance positioning | Avoid pending deeper review |

## Naming requirements

A candidate name SHOULD:

- identify the distinctive conversion of observed friction into an executable
  goalpost;
- avoid claiming scientific validation not yet achieved;
- avoid implying that all truth or all business logic is deterministic;
- remain meaningful without a specific model vendor, repository host, or test tool;
- distinguish the method from prompt engineering, spec-driven development, ordinary
  TDD, agent evaluation, and human-centred design;
- work as a method name, adjective, repository namespace, and spoken phrase;
- survive exact, near-match, acronym, domain, publication, product, and trademark
  screening;
- be understandable to engineering and research audiences without a metaphorical
  explanation.

## Working vocabulary to lock first

- Friction Record
- Authority Record
- Goalpost
- Scenario Fixture
- Persona Fixture
- Oracle
- Grader
- Red Proof

Naming the method before this vocabulary stabilizes risks selecting a label for the
wrong abstraction.

## Candidate directions for later screening

These are directions, not approved names:

| Direction | Strength | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| Friction-to-Goalpost Engineering | Closely describes the differentiating transformation; preliminary exact-phrase search found no clear methodology collision | Long; “friction” can sound negative without context |
| Goalpost-Evidence Development | Connects acceptance targets and proof | Awkward spoken form; collision search still required |
| Governed Goalpost Engineering | Emphasizes authority and lifecycle | Understates the observed-friction input |
| Acceptance-State Engineering | Emphasizes fail-closed release states | Sounds broader and more formal than current evidence supports |
| Evidence-Authorized Development | Captures “confidence has no authority” | Could be confused with existing evidence-based/evidence-gated work |

## Promotion gate for a name

Before public adoption:

- complete exact and semantic web/publication searches;
- search software packages, repositories, products, and conference proceedings;
- screen relevant domains and social handles;
- perform professional trademark clearance in intended jurisdictions;
- test comprehension with software engineers, research reviewers, and product leaders;
- confirm that reviewers infer the intended method rather than a broader unsupported
  claim;
- record the decision and rejected alternatives.
